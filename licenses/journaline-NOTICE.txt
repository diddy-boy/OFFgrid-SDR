Journaline news pages (DRM), from OFFgrid-SDR 2.3

OFFgrid-SDR's Journaline decoder is its own code, written to the Journaline
specification (ETSI TS 102 979). Its structure follows Fraunhofer IIS's
reference Journaline decoder, as published in the source of the Dream DRM
receiver, which is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2
or (at your option) any later version.

With thanks to Fraunhofer IIS for the reference decoder and the Journaline
service, and to the Dream project for publishing it.
